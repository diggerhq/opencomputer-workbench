## Overall Impression

Correct and consistent, but airless and heavy. The single biggest opportunity is a spacing cadence with three distinct steps and a weight system with one emphasis per context.

## Priority Issues

- **[P1] Monotonous rhythm.** 8 px inside everything, 24 px between everything; rows at 56 px hold a two-line stack with 4 px between the lines; headings sit 8 px above their cards. Fix: 8 / 12 / 16 / 32–40 steps, 20 px card inset, rows 64 px. Command: layout.
- **[P1] Weight mass.** Medium on row titles, section headings, status labels, tool names, speakers, keycaps, chips; semibold on the page title and wordmark. Fix: two weights, medium for one emphasis per context. Command: typeset.
- **[P2] Line density.** Thirteen row dividers, hairlines under two surface bands, a bordered composer footer band, chips behind stage words. Fix: tone and cadence separate; dividers only between turn groups. Command: polish.
- **[P2] Secondary chips at primary prominence.** Stage pills and keycaps carry the same weight as titles. Fix: plain muted text for the stage, regular keycaps. Command: polish.
- **[P3] Title competes with the mono repository/ref on one line.** Fix: repository and ref on the meta line at every width. Command: layout.

## Persona Red Flags

**Alex (Power User):** ⌘↵ exists for both composers; no keyboard traversal of the list; the archive control is hover-only at desktop.
**Sam (Accessibility):** the status badge renders twice per row (desktop column and mobile meta line), so a screen reader hears each state twice; everything else is labelled and the ring is visible.

## Minor Observations

The composer's footer band and the list's tab-bar hairline double-frame the top of the page. "Ended" rows keep the dot's space with no dot, by design.

## Questions to Consider

- Should the row's archive control be visible at rest on desktop, as it is on mobile?
- Does the list need the tab bar's surface band at all once rows have no dividers?
